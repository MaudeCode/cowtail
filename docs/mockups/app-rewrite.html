Rules

Both

  • This page is the visual spec for COW-9, COW-12, COW-13, COW-14, COW-16, COW-17, COW-18, COW-19, COW-21, COW-22, COW-32, COW-34, COW-59, COW-60, COW-62, COW-63 and COW-72. A ticket decides behaviour, data and API. This page decides layout, grouping, order and controls.
  • Colour and type are the Cowtail dark palette, Space Grotesk and DM Mono, from web/src/index.css and the iOS theme system. Light mode and the other iOS theme keep this layout with their own palette. The iOS figures draw light or dark from this page's appearance.
  • Sample data is invented. A glyph in the source-icon position stands for that source's uploaded icon.
  • A control the app has today, and that no ticket removes, stays in the nearest section.

Web

  • A section is a small uppercase mono label over rows split by hairlines. No card, panel or box encloses a section or a row. Sheets, popovers, dialogs, banners and notifications are the only enclosed surfaces.
  • Every action is an icon button with an accessible name and a tooltip. Text stays only on sign-in buttons, a question's answer options, a script action on an item, and the submit of a sheet or dialog.
  • A web list filters through one filter icon button. Search and pickers sit in the popup it opens, active filters show as removable chips, and the filter is part of the address.
  • Not drawn on web: the snooze picker is a popover from the Snooze button, Upcoming is a section after "Hermes is on it" on Home, and the maintenance window is a dialog from the wrench button with its banner above "Needs you".
  • Item detail stacks header, timeline with evidence, metric graph, details, fix, past occurrences. Incident detail stacks header, timeline, postmortem, members.
  • Below 960 px wide the web columns stack in source order, left column first, and a nav link keeps its label on the current page only. Below 600 px the top bar's cluster and hermes chips keep only their dots. Nothing scrolls sideways at 382 px.

iOS

  • iOS follows Apple's Human Interface Guidelines. Cowtail's identity stays in its palette, accent tint, app icon and typefaces, not in custom layout. A phone 393 pt wide is drawn 320 px wide, so 44 pt is 36 px.
  • Four tabs: Inbox, Cluster, Hermes, History. Settings is not a tab: a toolbar button on Inbox pushes it. Cluster health stays as the chip under the Inbox title.
  • Every tab is a NavigationStack with a navigation-bar title, large on a tab's root and inline once pushed. No screen hides the bar or draws its own title.
  • Content is a List or Form with system sections and headers, in the default inset-grouped style. Settings is an inset-grouped Form with Account, Notifications, Urgency overrides, Appearance, Devices, Developer and Links, in that order. A section's count sits in its header.
  • Page actions are toolbar items in the navigation bar: maintenance and Settings on Inbox, the filter on History, Add, Request postmortem and Resolve on an incident.
  • Per-row actions are swipe actions and a context menu. Rows carry no buttons, and a tap anywhere on a row opens it. A trailing full swipe acknowledges, and Snooze follows it; without Acknowledge there is no full swipe. A leading swipe approves or unsnoozes. A long press shows every action the server lists for the item, then its alert calls.
  • The item page is answer first: title and badges, then the details, root cause and action, then timeline, graph and past occurrences. Its actions sit in their own area pinned above the tab bar, never in the badge row.
  • The inbox holds Needs you, Hermes is on it and Upcoming, in that order, and hides an empty section. When Needs you is empty, an all-clear ContentUnavailableView takes its place. Other empty states, such as an empty History search, use ContentUnavailableView too. Handled items live in History.
  • Sheets use detents: medium for Snooze, large for a maintenance window, each with Cancel in its bar and a prominent submit. A reply or a note is a system alert with a text field.
  • With no session, the app's root is the sign-in screen instead of the tabs: Sign in with Apple and Pocket ID, then one skippable step that asks for notification permission, then the tabs. Signing out returns there.
  • Buttons use system styles at system sizes: .bordered, .borderedProminent for Approve and a sheet's submit, role: .destructive for Deny, Sign out and similar. Every control has a hit area of at least 44 × 44 pt.
  • In-content and toolbar actions are icon buttons with accessible names. Swipe actions, context-menu items, alert and sheet buttons, a Form's action rows such as Sign out, the sign-in and notification steps, and notification actions carry text labels as the system draws them. Text also stays on a question's answer options and a script action on an item.
  • Text and glyphs scale with Dynamic Type everywhere; nothing uses a fixed point size. A completed action plays success haptic feedback, and a refused one plays error feedback.
